All participants in projects, interviews, or recordings with our organization (or its affiliates) are expected to follow this Code of Conduct. Compliance is required to ensure a safe, respectful, and professional environment.

## 1. Respect
- Treat all individuals with dignity, respect, and courtesy.  
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior of any kind will not be tolerated.  

## 2. Professionalism
- Arrive prepared and on time for scheduled sessions.  
- Follow any instructions provided by organizers, producers, or hosts.  
- Ensure that all contributions (audio, video, written, or other materials) are accurate, lawful, and do not infringe on third-party rights.  

## 3. Safety and Privacy
- Do not share private or sensitive information about other participants without explicit consent.  
- Respect the boundaries and personal space of all participants.  
- Report any concerns about safety, harassment, or misconduct to the organizers immediately.  

## 4. Content Guidelines
- Contributions should be appropriate for the intended audience and context.  
- Avoid offensive, defamatory, or illegal content.  
- The organization reserves the right to remove or edit any content that violates these guidelines.  

## 5. Accountability
- Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in removal from the project or other appropriate actions.  
- Participants are responsible for understanding and following this Code of Conduct.  

## 6. Acceptance
By submitting the relevant contact/intake form, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Code of Conduct.
