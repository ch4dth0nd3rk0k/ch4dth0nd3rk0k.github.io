This document outlines the policy regarding compensation or honoraria for participants in projects, interviews, or recordings conducted by our organization (or its affiliates).

## 1. General Policy
- Participation in our projects is **voluntary**.  
- Compensation, if offered, will be described on a project-by-project basis.  
- The organization may offer **honoraria, stipends, or other forms of compensation** at its discretion.

## 2. Payment Details (Optional)
- Honoraria amounts, rates, or other compensation will be **communicated in advance** to participants.  
- Payment is not guaranteed unless explicitly agreed.  
- Participants are responsible for any taxes or legal obligations related to received compensation.

## 3. Methods of Payment
- Payment may be issued via approved methods (e.g., electronic transfer, PayPal, or other agreed-upon means).  
- Payment timelines will be communicated prior to participation or within a reasonable period after the project.

## 4. Waiver of Additional Claims
By participating, participants agree not to request additional compensation beyond what was explicitly communicated.  
No claim may be made for royalties, residuals, or other payment unless specifically agreed in writing.

## 5. Acceptance
By submitting the relevant contact/intake form, participants acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to these Honorarium / Compensation Guidelines.
