## Introduction
The type of person we will be discussing in this article is essentially everything I am looking to avoid: *combative*, *hostile*, has a *power complex*, etc .... But it wasn't obvious at first exactly who this person was, and what was their motivation. In fact everything started rather *cordial* and mature:


    
![png](/assets/images/2025-01-11-dating-wars-challenger_files/2025-01-11-dating-wars-challenger_5_0.png)
    


Ever notice how people are on their best behavior when they want something? She is clearly capable of communicating sufficiently and at least presenting herself with maturity. So I went to look at the [contact form](https://chadthunderkok.com/dating-contact/) that she submitted, and that is really when the first **red flag** made itself known.

## The Mask Slips
Other than her *political affiliation* being **liberal** (which I would expect is very unlikely to match with me), her submission was agreeable. It was when I arrived at the *"essay"* portion of the form that I started to suspect this individual is not going to match. Here are her two responses:

**Ideal Match Essay**

    I’m looking for a genuine connection, someone to have adventures with to have
    intelligent conversations with who challenges me and doesn’t bore me to tears.


**Our Match Essay**

    I am intrigued by your honesty and respect that you’re not afraid to say what
    you want regardless of how “off putting” it might seem, it implies you’re
    confident and know what you want which I find admirable. I feel like you would
    challenge me in a good way.


Now there are two *main issues* that stick out in these essays:
+ the desire to be *"challenged"*
+ no mention of what she offers (only what I can do for her)

People tend to easily betray what is their intention. It's not something that can be hidden indefinitely. In this instance there is some very clear fixation on being *"challenged"*, and at the same time this individual lacks the capacity to consider what she *"brings to the table."* That lack of awareness, a lack of understanding that you **must give** in order **to get**, is already indicative that this person is not interested in [collaboration](https://chadthunderkok.com/blog/2024/08/03/the-nature-of-romance) (i.e. working as a team). But what specifically is the hang up with being **challenged**?

## The Ultimate Wing Man
So at this point I started thinking I needed to *consult* with a higher power: **ChatGPT**. I sent my digital *"wing man"* the text from the contact form essays and out of everything they responded with this stuck out the most:

    ChatGPT: Explore what "challenging in a good way" means to them—this could
    reveal how they approach conflict, growth, or differing perspectives.


So I did, and I asked her about *"what do you mean by challenge"* to which she responded as follows:

    Her: Well, I can be a bit mouthy sometimes and despite my tiny height I can be
    pretty intimidating and most guys just kind of roll over and take it. Which is
    boring. I feel like you’ll actually engage me and call me on my bullshit.


Now if you know anything about me, which if you've seen any of my other articles or content, you could probably guess that this is not going to match well with yours truly. Here was my response to her:

    Me: You sound combative ... Like you don't know your place ....


Now in **hindsight** I should have already stopped engaging with this individual ... but that's one of the main problems with *online dating*: it's easy to get the **wrong impression**. And in this instance I was still trying to piece together who this person was. Her response was truly the *chef's kiss* of the whole conversation thus far:

    Her: Hahahahaha my place is wherever I want it to be babes.


Now to clarify, when I say *"you don't know your place"*, my point isn't to be *combative* or derogatory, though I can appreciate how it could come off that way. What I mean is that this person is supposedly *"seeking genuine connection"* (**literally** her words from the essays) but has this strange *antagonistic* and *hostile* demeanor and attitude towards her past relationships and dating in general. As if she is under some **delusion** that people are going to be *"interested"* in spending their time fighting with some unhinged individual ... that is extremely immature and possibly one of the worst red flags I have ever seen or can even conceive of.

Also keep in mind this individual **messaged me**. I did not reach out to contact them, they wanted something **from me**. Then they proceed to talk all about how they are *"mouthy"* and *"intimidating"* (though short). You are in a position where you want something from me, and you proceed to literally make yourself look like the most abysmal, annoying, draining, miserable, headache of a person. This is like the equivalent of some customer walking into a store and believing that, even though they have bought nothing, they are entitled to speak and act however they want. Let's make this clear:

    When you submit a contact form to me, you are in my territory in my house. You
    obey my rules or you will be removed.


There is no *humility* or *gratitude* in this individual. Just a sheer need to fight (because they feel so massively insecure from being constantly rejected in the past). But wait there is more. I sent one final message:

    Me: I don't think you understand what you're getting into here ... or what
    matches with me.


Which I might add is extremely true: this person doesn't have the slightest clue (or motivation to know) who I am, what I am about, what type of relationship I value, and who matches me. Naturally she had more to say (because clearly she lacks the humility to recognize where she isn't wanted and should bow out gracefully):

The lack of *self-awareness* of this individual is one of the worst I have encountered so far. I mean this is a walking *nightmare* of a person, someone you don't ever want to *"handle."* And to any of you reading this who express similar sentiment let's set a few things straight:

Don't get it twisted. What is the value of *"dominating"* some low quality person? What do they have to gain? A headache? Wasted time? Do I need to go wage a war over nothing? You are the *paragon* of insufferable, and no **real man** (i.e. a mature male that respects himself and has confidence and self-esteem) would ever, and I mean **EVER**, have anything to do with you.

## Moral
You can't form an *alliance* with an enemy. And *alliances* are what healthy and fulfilling relationships are all about. There is a mutual respect required, not some immature power struggle where one or both party feels insecure and now needs to seek *"revenge"* in order to secure themselves. Phrases like **"put me in my place"** or **"challenge me"** (I would *strongly* suspect and ChatGPT seems to agree) are correlated with individuals with deep unresolved emotional issues, and who are not ready for a serious adult relationship (and *especially* not the kind of *collaborative* relationship built on mutual respect that I am seeking). But while this was not an overly pleasant experience, it was necessary in order for me to become more clear about who I am, what I value, and what kind of relationship I am seeking.
